# India-specific clinical gaps to validate

- multilingual and low-literacy education without changing clinical meaning;
- intermittent connectivity and store-and-forward workflows;
- affordability, device availability, and calibration of home measurements;
- public/private care transitions and referral closure;
- ABDM consent, health-record exchange, and local data-governance requirements;
- India-specific medication names, formulations, availability, and adherence barriers;
- age-, sex-, pregnancy-, and comorbidity-specific definitions;
- TB, malaria, dengue, anaemia, undernutrition, and other program-linked pathways;
- clinician workload, escalation ownership, and emergency fallback;
- evaluation datasets that are legally usable and representative without exposing PHI.

These are product and validation questions, not assumptions to encode into a model. Each gap needs an owner, evidence source, and acceptance test before it affects a disease pack.
