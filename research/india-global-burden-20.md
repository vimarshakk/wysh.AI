# India/global burden research register

This file records the research obligation, not verified epidemiology. Planning figures supplied in the mandate are deliberately not promoted to runtime data because they use mixed years, denominators, age ranges, and definitions. Every condition needs source-level review before any number is published or used for prioritization.

## Required review fields

1. India estimate, year, geography, population, numerator/denominator, case definition, and uncertainty.
2. Global estimate with the same metadata.
3. Incidence/prevalence/mortality distinction.
4. Care-gap evidence and relevance to longitudinal monitoring.
5. Source provenance, license/reuse status, and verification date.
6. Whether the evidence supports product prioritization only or any clinical behavior; default is product prioritization only.

The 20-condition scope is represented in `disease-priority-scorecard.csv` and the engineering registry. Scores are provisional engineering scores, not disease-burden estimates.
