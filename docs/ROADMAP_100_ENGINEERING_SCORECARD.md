## Adoption policy

The project shortlist is governed by five decisions: `INTEGRATED`, `ADAPT`, `REFERENCE`, `DEFER`, and `REJECT`. No upstream repository is copied into wysh.AI merely to increase adoption count. Dependencies, model weights, terminology distributions, and clinical content require separate license, provenance, security, resource, and validation gates.

Existing WyshCare modules remain canonical. This workspace has not modified WyshCare, production systems, protected branches, or patient data.

## Current blockers to 100/100

- Independent clinical validation with adequate, rights-cleared, representative data.
- Imaging model inference, modality benchmarks, model-weight licensing, and diagnostic safety evaluation.
- Regulatory risk management and operational readiness.
- Explicit authorization from the owner before any WyshCare development-repository change.

The correct target is evidence-complete engineering plus formally approved clinical and operational gates—not an inflated percentage produced by installing open-source projects.
