# R16F independent readiness review

The final readiness review must be performed by people independent of the
implementation and must examine R16A–R16E evidence, unresolved risks, protocol
freeze, statistical analysis plan, reference standards, approvals, resources,
rollback/stop controls, and auditability.

The reviewer must issue exactly one decision:

- `GO`: all mandatory evidence is verified and the study may begin under the
  approved scope.
- `NO-GO`: one or more mandatory evidence items are missing, unverified,
  expired, or contradicted.

R16 engineering records `NO-GO` until the external evidence exists. No code,
synthetic test, or model result can substitute for a qualified independent
readiness decision.
