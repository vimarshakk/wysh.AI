# R15 independent review and adjudication

## Reviewer requirements

Future validation requires documented qualifications relevant to the intended
use, clinical domain, statistics, data privacy, and safety review. Reviewers
must be independent of model implementation decisions and disclose conflicts of
interest before access to evaluation material.

## Review process

1. Lock the protocol, model/rule versions, data manifest, rubric, and analysis
   plan.
2. Assign blinded synthetic or approved study cases through controlled access.
3. Record reviewer-level labels, rationale, confidence, abstention, and time.
4. Reconcile disagreements using a prespecified adjudicator and retain both
   original and adjudicated decisions.
5. Version and hash the final analysis package; never overwrite an audit event.

No reviewer identity, qualification, approval, or adjudication is asserted by
this engineering artifact. Those fields remain unfulfilled until qualified
people and governance approvals are formally appointed.
