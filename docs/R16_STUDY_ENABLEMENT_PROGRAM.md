# R16 study-enablement program

R16 is a controlled preparation framework. It does not recruit reviewers,
request datasets, submit to an ethics body, make regulatory representations, or
authorize a clinical study. Those actions require explicit owner approval and
qualified external participants.

## Workstreams

- R16A: verify reviewer qualifications, independence, responsibilities, and
  conflict-of-interest declarations.
- R16B: verify lawful dataset access, permitted use, provenance, security, and
  retention/deletion controls.
- R16C: lock clinician-approved reference standards and adjudication methods.
- R16D: complete qualified ethics, privacy, and regulatory applicability review.
- R16E: freeze protocol, statistical analysis plan, model/rule versions,
  monitoring, and stop rules.
- R16F: conduct an independent readiness review and record GO/NO-GO.

Every workstream is represented as an evidence record. Missing, unsigned,
unverified, or expired evidence must keep study readiness `NO`.
