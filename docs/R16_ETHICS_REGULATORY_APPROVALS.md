# R16D ethics and regulatory pathway

R16 provides an applicability-assessment template only. A qualified privacy,
research-ethics, and regulatory team must determine which approvals apply to a
specific intended use, jurisdiction, dataset, study design, and software
function. This is not legal advice or a regulatory classification.

Before study initiation, record the responsible sponsor, ethics/Institutional
review pathway, privacy and consent position, data-use permissions, safety and
risk classification, applicable Indian requirements, submission status,
conditions, approval dates, and expiry/revocation controls.

No ethics approval, regulatory assessment, real-data permission, or study
authorization is present in R16.
