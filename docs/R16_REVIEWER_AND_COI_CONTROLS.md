# R16A reviewer and conflict-of-interest controls

Before any study, each reviewer record must include identity verification,
domain qualifications, role and responsibilities, independence assessment,
conflict-of-interest declaration, confidentiality agreement, training status,
and approval/expiry dates. The study owner must verify the evidence through a
controlled process.

The model implementation team cannot self-approve clinical reference labels or
clinical effectiveness. Reviewers must be independent of the outcome being
assessed, and disagreements require a prespecified adjudicator.

No reviewer recruitment or qualification is asserted by this artifact.
