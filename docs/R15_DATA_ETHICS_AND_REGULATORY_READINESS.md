# R15 data, ethics, and regulatory readiness

R15 records prerequisites only. It does not determine legal status, grant
ethics approval, or authorize access to non-synthetic data.

Before any future study, the sponsor must document the lawful basis and consent
position, data-use agreements, provenance and licensing, data minimization,
de-identification or pseudonymization controls, retention/deletion, access
roles, incident response, cross-border processing position, and privacy impact
assessment.

An ethics/Institutional review pathway must be identified before recruitment or
secondary use of health data. An India-specific regulatory assessment must be
performed by qualified counsel and regulatory professionals, including the
applicable medical-device/software, privacy, clinical-research, and institutional
requirements for the exact intended use. This document is not a regulatory
classification or legal opinion.

No such approvals, agreements, or real-data access are present in R15.
